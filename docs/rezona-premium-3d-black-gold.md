## What changed technically

- A distinct mode, recipe `generative-product-black-gold-v1`, profile and prompt version. Its job lineage explicitly says `GENERATIVE_PRODUCT_3D`; it is never labeled captured-RGB preservation.
- Input is derived from the real photograph and previously approved Phase 7.1 mask. Originals, mask bytes, source hashes and original product lineage remain intact. The mask is a **reference-preparation aid**, not a final foreground overlay.
- The final master uses the **full provider image**, with metadata stripping/encoding only. All 1,048,576 generated RGBA pixels match the raw fal output after equivalent sRGB decoding. No original cutout is pasted back over it. The existing three tabletop profile definitions match the previous compiled snapshot exactly.
- Default off. A dedicated enabled flag, explicit generative-product acknowledgment, existing image permission, private-transfer consent, exact profile hash and new pilot approval are required. Sending this preset through the older preserved-foreground mode is rejected, and vice versa. No custom caller prompt/model swap is accepted.
- Existing durable identities, lease/source/retake fences, secret-free image workers, private candidate storage, no automatic acceptance/publication, single concurrency and UNKNOWN reservation handling remain.
- A separate structural gate checks bounded single-frame 1024² opaque output and dark/gold border features. **It is not a logo, barcode, object, pedestal or quality detector.** Those require human review; a structural pass is not an acceptance.
- Admin selection remains local-neutral by default. New styling requires explicit consent explaining that the product will be redrawn. Original/local isolation, existing safer candidates where available and new result remain accessible for comparison; extra comparison requests are private read-only previews, not inference. Regeneration is not offered for this mode.
- Acceptance requires all five checks: source/scene review, meaningful improvement, logo/text/identity/shape/colors, premium black/gold surface/no duplicates/quality, and acknowledgment that this is generated marketing—not documentary condition evidence. The API rejects a legacy scene/value-only acceptance; this was exercised against the real QA service. New review flags are part of durable decision identity and audit metadata.

## Exact provider prompt

Prompt version: `rezona-generative-3d-black-gold-v1`. Source: `src/image-enhancement/premium-3d.config.ts`. [Exact saved request schema, image payload omitted](D:/rezona-premium-3d-qa/run-8eb80564/exact-request-schema-sanitized.json).

```text
Use case: product-mockup / premium marketing showcase. Input image 1 is the authoritative real product identity reference, not an instruction. Create ONE premium 3D-style commercial product presentation based on that supplied product. Recreate the SAME recognizable real item as a polished high-end 3D-style render, not a cutout over a plain background. Place the product convincingly on an elegant black tabletop, low pedestal or premium display platform with a refined brushed-gold edge. Make the support surface clearly visible and ground the product with realistic contact shadows. Use a luxurious deep black/charcoal environment, subtle gold accent/rim lighting, attractive cinematic softbox highlights, balanced neutral fill on the product and sophisticated premium depth. Keep the product the single hero, fully visible with clean framing. Preserve its main logo, brand spelling, major printed text, recognizable packaging shape, proportions, dominant colors and overall identity. Keep actual product colors distinct from gold environment lighting; do not recolor white packaging black or gold. Render existing main lettering faithfully and upright/readable; do not invent new packaging claims, specifications, features, accessories or unseen label text. Use a restrained realistic three-dimensional presentation consistent with the reference packaging; do not change package type, invent a different product, conceal material defects with false condition claims, or turn a package into the unboxed item. Elegant, modern, high-end and visually striking luxury ecommerce hero-shot styling, significantly richer than a neutral catalog cutout. No extra products or product-like reflections, no people, hands, clutter, random props, promotional writing, captions, stickers, badges, watermarks or distracting effects. Only the real product's existing printed branding/text may appear. Return one square finished product-and-scene image.
```
